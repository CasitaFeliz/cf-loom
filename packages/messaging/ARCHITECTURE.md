# Internals of @cf-loom/messaging

How `messaging` (see [README.md](README.md)) is split into modules, as [Package layout](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#package-layout) in the root ARCHITECTURE.md requires, and how long it waits for a Handler. The Broker interface and its adapters are in [Runtime](https://github.com/CasitaFeliz/cf-loom/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md#runtime) in the root ARCHITECTURE.md; what the Broker must hold is in [Applying the Broker plan](README.md#applying-the-broker-plan).

## Modules

The entry `src/index.ts` (`.`) re-exports from `broker/`, `consuming/`, `sending/` and `messaging/`; `memory/index.ts` re-exports `in-memory/`, and `jetstream/index.ts` re-exports `jetstream-broker/` and `broker-limits/`'s `Duration`, and `sqs/index.ts` re-exports `sqs-broker/` and `broker-limits/`'s `Duration`. The wire rules of [Over the Broker](https://github.com/CasitaFeliz/cf-loom/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md#over-the-broker), a subject, the Stream named after a Service, a Durable consumer's name, a Broker prefix and the names and subjects it prefixes, the Broker message id, the headers cf-loom sets, which Fact ids and Transport headers travel unchanged, the bytes a Message's Broker headers take and the most JetStream stores, reading them back, the SNS/SQS wire in `wire/sns-attributes.ts`: the names of the SNS message attributes a Message travels in, the ASCII JSON its ids and Transport headers are written in, and reading them back, and a body's JSON from bytes and its bytes from JSON, live in `@cf-loom/contract`'s `wire/`, and the Broker plan the CLI prints, and reading one back, in its `broker-plan/`; the runs of the Message Handlers and the Handler map's lookup live in `@cf-loom/handlers`'s `./internal`.

## A Handler's wait

The core waits for a Handler (see [Message Handlers](README.md#message-handlers)) as long as the Broker waits for the Delivery's acknowledgement, the `waitMs` the adapter hands it (JetStream: the ack wait, or with a backoff that Delivery's entry; SNS/SQS: the queue's visibility timeout, or that Delivery's entry of its backoff tag; both read at `consume`), so the organization's setting per Durable consumer bounds every Handler and no number of cf-loom's own does. Past it the Handler's signal aborts, the Delivery settles pending, and the Broker, whose wait passed too, delivers it again; the run goes on detached. A wait longer than a Node timer holds is timed in steps. Each Durable consumer is pulled one Message at a time, so no Message waits in the Service while its wait runs out.
