# Internals of @cf-loom/messaging

How `messaging` (see [README.md](README.md)) is split into modules, as [Package layout](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#package-layout) in the root ARCHITECTURE.md requires, and keeps a Message in flight while its Handler runs. The broker interface and its adapters are in [Runtime](https://github.com/CasitaFeliz/cf-loom/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md#runtime) in the root ARCHITECTURE.md; what the Broker must hold is in [Applying the plan](README.md#applying-the-plan).

## Modules

The entry `src/index.ts` (`.`) re-exports from `broker/`, `sending/` and `messaging/`; `memory/index.ts` re-exports `in-memory/`, and `jetstream/index.ts` re-exports `jetstream-broker/`. The wire rules of [Over the Broker](https://github.com/CasitaFeliz/cf-loom/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md#over-the-broker), a subject, the stream named after a Service, a durable consumer's name, a prefix and the names and subjects it prefixes, the broker message id, the headers cf-loom sets and a body's JSON from bytes and its bytes from JSON, live in `@cf-loom/contract`'s `wire/`, and the broker plan the CLI prints, and reading one back, in its `broker-plan/`; the runs of the Message Handlers and the Handler map's lookup live in `@cf-loom/handlers`'s `./internal`.

| Module | Holds | Depends on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `broker/` | the broker interface an adapter implements, `Broker` and its parts, and `ConsumedFault`, which a `Settlement` carries | none |
| `subjects/` | where each Message of a Contract travels, computed once at `messaging()`: the Service's name and its stream; its Events' live versions in version order and its Commands' Active versions, with their subjects; each consumed Subscription and Command Version not Retired with its key in the Handler map, subject, stream and durable consumer; a root that is no Contract, or a Subscription whose Event no list holds, throws | none |
| `sending/` | `publish` and `send` and their types: every body checked with its version's schema, by the Message body check `@cf-loom/handlers` shares, before any is built, the Fact id, the transport headers checked against the names cf-loom sets, each `OutgoingMessage`, sending one at a time; `PublishError` and `InvalidMessageError`, and the Fact id and header rules `deliver` shares | `broker`, `subjects` |
| `consuming/` | the consumers of one `consume`: each Handler looked up as `serve` looks one up, a stub left out with a warning; one delivery read as JSON, run with its Handler's Reader and settled; a `ConsumedFault` handed to `onServiceFault` or the console, its text lines, which the console and `settled()` share, `aboutOf`, what names the Element and the Message in a line, and the line for a `receive` that rejected, which `settled()` reports | `broker`, `subjects` |
| `in-memory/` | the in-memory Broker: one connection; the Messages it takes recorded, each broker message id once for its lifetime, and delivered by one line, one at a time, to the consumers of their subject; `deliver`, writing a body back through each consumer's Reader by the write-back `@cf-loom/handlers` shares; `pending`, with the `Delivery` each consumer left unacknowledged, and `redeliver`; `settled()`, its failures and the 1,000-delivery cut | `broker`, `sending`, `consuming` |
| `jetstream-broker/` | the JetStream adapter: the prefix checked once; the Service's `connect` called once for each `messaging()`; its stream checked to hold every subject it publishes and sends, by asking the server which streams hold each, the connection drained on a refusal; each Message published on its prefixed subject with its broker headers, its acknowledgement awaited, a duplicate counted as sent; `close` draining the connection, which a closed connection is spared and one the Service is draining is waited for; and `apply`: the plan, its prefix and the limits checked before it connects, each stream's and durable consumer's settings resolved from its own limits over the defaults, then on its own connection each stream created or given the subjects it does not hold and its limits, before each durable consumer is created or updated, stopping at the first refusal, a NATS refusal named by what it refused, the connection drained at the end | `broker` |
| `messaging/` | `messaging()` and its types, connecting through the Broker, telling it the Service's name, its stream and the subjects it publishes and sends, and wiring the subjects, the publisher, the sender and `consume` to that one connection; the life of a `Messaging`, open, closing, closed, `consume` once, and what is in flight, the Handlers running and any `publish`, `send` or `consume`, which the core keeps, not the adapter, so `close()` waits for it before it closes the connection, and a delivery while closing is left pending | `broker`, `subjects`, `sending`, `consuming` |

## In-progress acknowledgements

While a Handler runs (see [Message Handlers](README.md#message-handlers)), the adapter sends in-progress acknowledgements at a fraction of the durable consumer's ack wait, read from it at `consume`, so a slow Handler's Message is not delivered to a sibling instance meanwhile.
