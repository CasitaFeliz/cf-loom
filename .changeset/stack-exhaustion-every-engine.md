A value nested past the call stack is one `malformed` Issue at its root in every engine, and no other throw is read as one. In Firefox, whose overflow is an `InternalError`, the client returns the `INVALID_OUTCOME` Fault or its `INVALID_INPUT` Rejection instead of throwing on such a value. A getter or Proxy trap of a value a Caller or a Service builds that throws a `RangeError` for another reason, such as `Invalid array length`, throws as any other error does, instead of being read as `The value is nested too deeply to read`.
