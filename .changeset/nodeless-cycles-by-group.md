`generate` refuses a cycle through `z.lazy()`, unions and intersections alone in time that grows with the recursive schemas, instead of its square: a Contract with 8,000 recursive unions, intersections or aliases of each other took 6.5 s in the walk alone, and doubling it took about five times longer. A schema's own failure is judged when it is written, and the cycles are judged among the schemas still standing when the set of recursive schemas closes, so a cycle through a failing schema is told only by that schema's line, and each other cycle of the set at its first schema, the one the walk reaches first. The line of a cycle may therefore sit at another schema than before, lines may come in another order, and a cycle that another cycle among the same recursive schemas used to hide is told. A recursive schema that admits null now always gets its own `.meta({ id }) on a schema that admits null` line, replacing or joining the cycle lines. A Contract that `generate` accepts is accepted with the same Snapshot.
