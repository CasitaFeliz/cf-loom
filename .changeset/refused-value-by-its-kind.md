A refusal shows a value that untyped code passed and its type does not allow by its kind, running none of the value's code. A limits setting or an `account` that `inProcess()`, `applyBrokerPlan` and `sqs()` refuse is shown as `util.inspect` prints it only when it is a string, a number, a boolean, `null`, `undefined` or a list held as data (its first 100 entries, three levels deep): an object reads `an object`, a function `a function`, a Symbol `a symbol`, a bigint `a bigint` and any other list `a list`, so a refusal no longer prints every key of a wide object, a Symbol's whole description or a bigint's every digit, or throws what the value's `[inspect.custom]`, getter or Proxy trap threw in place of its own line naming the place; an object that used to print its keys (`{ seconds: 30 }`) now reads `an object`. A validation line names a function, a Symbol, a bigint or a revoked Proxy by its kind too, where it printed a function's source or a Symbol's whole description, and where a throwing `Symbol.toPrimitive` or a revoked Proxy threw out of `generate` and `serve`. A Rejection's `code` and a returned `kind` that are an object, a function, a list, a Symbol or a bigint are named by their kind in the `InvalidOutcomeError` and the Fault's `cause` of a Handler, instead of `[object Object]`, a function's source or what the value's `toString` threw. `fault({ retryAfter: 1n })` and `serve({ maxBodyBytes: 1n })` refuse with `got a bigint`, where they said `got 1`, the text of a valid number.
